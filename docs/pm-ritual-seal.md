# PM Ritual Seal

A Ritual Seal means the project accepts the validated and peer-reviewed contribution.

Eligible PM reviewers are project creators, explicitly scoped project managers/members, delegated PM reviewers, or admins. A global project-manager role does not grant authority over unrelated projects.

The contributor cannot seal her own submission by default. Single-person projects require delegation or admin review unless a future explicit policy permits self-seal.

Actions:

- Apply Ritual Seal.
- Request changes.
- Reject.
- Recuse or delegate.

The seal creates an accepted-pending-settlement outcome only. It does not complete the task, release rewards, activate dependencies, or publish stories.
